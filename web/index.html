    Tips

    • Stand so your upper body and both hands are in the frame, like the signers in the Slovo videos.
    • Sign one word at a time and pause briefly between words. The model recognizes isolated signs, not continuous sentences.
    • Even, bright lighting and a plain background help.
    • "—" means the model sees no sign.
